About BlueObsidian.

Our Story

Building Tomorrow's Workforce.

BlueObsidian IO was founded with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between emerging technology and workforce readiness. Based in Detroit, Michigan, we recognized the growing demand for skilled drone pilots and software developers — and the lack of accessible, high-quality training programs to meet that demand.

With over 5 years of professional experience in UAS operations and a proven track record across 6+ training organizations, our founder set out to create a workforce development company that combines expert instruction with innovative technology.

Today, BlueObsidian IO serves high schools, community colleges, law enforcement agencies, and individual students seeking to launch careers in these rapidly growing fields. Our proprietary training app and hands-on approach have resulted in a 98% FAA Part 107 exam pass rate — one of the highest in the industry.
