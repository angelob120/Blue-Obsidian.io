What you never pay

No subscription. No cut of your sales.

Most ways of selling online cost you a monthly fee, a cut of every sale, or both. Zero Monthly costs you neither. The whole platform is funded by a small fee on the customer's order instead.

No hardware needed

Orders arrive by email on the phone or laptop you already own, which is the finished product and costs nothing. If you want a screen by the pass, use a spare tablet or buy the pair we recommend from Amazon for about $75 - we do not resell them and make nothing on them.

No monthly subscription

You are not renting software. There is no recurring bill to us, this month or any month.

No cut of your sales

We take no percentage of your sales. A $500 day and a $50 day cost you the same: nothing.

No setup fee and no contract

Build your menu, paste your link, start taking orders. Nothing to pay to get going, and nothing to cancel: export your customer list and walk away whenever you want.

Card fees are yours, the same as they are today. No contracts and no setup fee.

How the customer service fee works

The fee is 3.5% of the food subtotal, never less than $1.00 and never more than $20.00. It scales with the order, so small ones stay in noise territory and only large ones carry a real fee. It appears as its own line at checkout, never folded into your item prices.
