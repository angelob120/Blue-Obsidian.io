3. What we use it for

  • Answering your enquiry, and following it up.
  • Enrolling you in a program, and running that program.
  • Sending you the course information, schedules and reminders that go with an enrollment.
  • Sending the text messages described in section 6a, if you opted in and only then.
  • Keeping records we are required to keep, and defending ourselves if a dispute arises.
  • Understanding which parts of the site people find useful.

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

4. Why we are allowed to

We rely on your consent for marketing texts and marketing emails, on the performance of a contract for anything to do with a course you enrolled in, on our legitimate interest in running and improving the business for site usage and for replying to an enquiry, and on legal obligation for the records tax and education rules require us to keep.

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing it is as easy as giving it. See section 6a for texts and section 9 for everything else.

5. Who else sees it

We do not sell your personal information, and we have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding twelve months. We do not trade, rent, or hand your details to anyone for their own marketing.

The only outside parties that touch your information are the service providers that run parts of this business for us: the platform that hosts this website, the customer relationship and messaging platform that carries our contact form, chat widget, emails and texts, and our payment processor when you pay for a course. Each acts on our written instructions, may use your information only to do that work for us, and may not use it for anything of their own.

We also disclose information when the law requires it, when we have to establish or defend a legal claim, or to protect someone from harm. If the business is ever sold or merged, your information may transfer with it, and this policy travels with the information.

6a. Mobile numbers and text messages

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers, or consent to be messaged, with any third party for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with anyone outside of delivering the messages you asked for.

If you gave us your mobile number on the contact form at https://blueobsidian.io/contact.html or in the chat widget on this site, and ticked the consent box, we use it to reply to you and to send you the messages described in the Text Messaging Terms at https://blueobsidian.io/sms.html. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

What we collect when you opt in, and nothing beyond it:

  • Your mobile number, and your name and email address if you gave them on the same form.
  • The record of your consent: the wording you agreed to, the page you agreed on, and the date and time. We keep this because consent has to be provable, not asserted.
  • The messages sent to and from that number, and whether they were delivered.
  • Your opt-out, if you send one, so that we do not contact you again by mistake.

The only third parties that see your number are the providers that deliver the message and hold our contact records for us. They act on our instructions only. Ask us and we delete your number. Unsubscribing keeps only the record that you unsubscribed, so that we do not contact you again by mistake.

8. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, and ask us to stop using it for marketing. We do not charge for any of this and we do not treat you differently for asking.

If you are in California, the CCPA gives you the rights to know, to delete, to correct, to opt out of sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined, so there is nothing to opt out of, but the right to know and to delete work exactly as described here.

To exercise any of it, email angelobrown1000@gmail.com or call (313) 671-3685. We answer within thirty days. We may need to confirm who you are before we hand over a copy of somebody's personal information, which is a protection for you rather than an obstacle.

9. Stopping marketing

  • Texts: reply STOP to any message. See the Text Messaging Terms for the full list of keywords.
  • Emails: use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any marketing email, or ask us.
  • Calls: tell us on any call, or email us, and we stop.

Stopping marketing does not stop the messages you need about a course you are enrolled in, such as a room change or a cancelled session. You can ask us to stop those too, but then you have to check the schedule yourself.

10. Security, children, and other countries

We hold your information on services that encrypt it in transit and at rest, and we limit who inside the company can see it to the people who need it to do their job. No system is perfect, and we will tell you promptly if something happens that puts your information at real risk.

Our programs are aimed at adults and at students enrolled through their school. We do not knowingly collect information from a child under 13 through this website. Where a school or agency enrolls a minor, we take that information from the institution under our agreement with them, not from the student.

We are based in the United States and your information is processed there.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we update the date at the top of the page. A change that widens what we do with your information beyond what is described here needs fresh consent from you rather than a new version of this page.
