Cookies and Local Storage

Every cookie and browser value we set, what it is for, and how long it lasts. Advertising cookies are used on this site only, never on a restaurant's ordering page, and only if you accept them.

Blue Obsidian · Last updated August 25, 2026

On this page
  1. 1. The short version
  2. 2. What we set
  3. 3. Turning them off

1. The short version

Two different pages, two different answers. On a restaurant's ordering page we set no advertising cookies and run no third-party trackers at all - only our own visit counter, and only if you accept it. On this site, where we advertise to restaurant owners, we also use Google and Meta advertising cookies to see which ads work, and those load only after you press Accept. Declining leaves nothing but the cookies that keep you signed in.

3. Turning them off

Your browser can block or clear all of these. Blocking the essential ones will sign you out and empty your cart; blocking the analytics value only means a restaurant counts your visits separately rather than together. To change an answer you already gave, use the Cookie choices link in the footer - on this site and on a restaurant's ordering page alike. It clears the answer and asks again on the spot, and declining stops the advertising cookies loading from that moment on.

We honor Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends that signal, the answer on this site is already no: the advertising cookies above do not load, and they stay off unless you deliberately press Accept on the notice, which says plainly that your browser had already declined. Nothing on a restaurant's ordering page is ever shared for advertising, whatever your browser sends.
