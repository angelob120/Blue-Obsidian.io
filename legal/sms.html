Text Messaging Terms

1. The short version

We text and call only the number you typed into the form on this site and asked us to use. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. We do not sell or share your mobile number, or the fact that you opted in, with anyone.

Blue Obsidian is operated by blueobsidian io LLC. These terms cover messages we send from our own number to people who opted in on this website. They do not cover anything a restaurant using our software does with its own customers.

Privacy Policy: https://blueobsidian.io/legal/privacy. Terms of Service: https://blueobsidian.io/legal/terms. This page is at https://blueobsidian.io/legal/sms.

2. What we send

One program, aimed at restaurant owners and managers who asked us to get in touch. The messages are conversational and promotional: we are following up on an enquiry about our ordering software.

  • A reply to the question you asked in the form, from a person.
  • Help getting set up - what to do next, and an offer to walk you through it.
  • Occasional news about the product, pricing, and features we have added.
  • A reminder if you started an account and did not finish it.

That is the whole program. We do not send messages on behalf of anyone else, we do not sell your number as a lead, and we do not text people who did not fill in the form. There is no age-gated, loan, gambling, cannabis, or high-risk content of any kind.

3. How you agree to receive them

You agree in one place and one place only: the chat widget on our website, https://blueobsidian.io. The widget appears on every page of the site, and it is the only place we ever ask for a mobile number. You enter your name and mobile number, type your question, and tick the consent box. The box is never pre-ticked, and no number is ever added by us, imported from a list, bought, or entered on your behalf.

The wording beside that checkbox reads, in full:

By submitting, you authorize blueobsidian io LLC to text/call the number above for promotional messages, possibly using automated means. Msg/data rates apply, msg frequency varies. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See terms at https://blueobsidian.io/legal/sms and privacy policy at https://blueobsidian.io/legal/privacy. Text HELP for help and STOP to unsubscribe.

We record the wording you agreed to, the date and time, and the page you were on, and we keep that record for as long as we hold your number. It is what we would produce if anyone ever asked us to prove you asked to hear from us.

Consent is not a condition of purchase. You can use the site, create an account, and pay us without agreeing to any text message, and refusing costs you nothing.

4. How often, and what it costs

Message frequency varies. In practice it is a handful of messages while we answer your question, and rarely more than four a month after that. You receive nothing until you opt in.

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge you anything for these messages. Your mobile carrier may charge you according to your own plan.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on your carrier, your plan and your handset, and we cannot guarantee any particular message arrives. Nothing you are owed by us depends on a text arriving - if you would rather be emailed, say so and we will.

5. Stopping them, and getting help

Reply STOP to any message from us and the messages stop. We send one confirmation and then nothing further. STOP works at any time, from any message, and costs you nothing beyond whatever your carrier charges to send a text.

  • STOP, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE or QUIT - unsubscribes you. One confirmation follows, then silence.
  • HELP or INFO - replies with who we are and how to reach us.
  • START or UNSTOP - resubscribes you, if you stopped and changed your mind.

You can also stop them by emailing hello@blueobsidian.io and asking, and we will do it for you. Unsubscribing from texts does not close your account, cancel anything you have bought, or stop the emails you need about your account.

6. What we do with your number

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number, or your consent to be messaged, with any third party for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with anyone outside of delivering these messages, and are never passed to another company as a lead.

Your number is used to reply to you, to help you set up, and to send you the messages described above. The only third parties that see it are LeadConnector, which runs the chat widget and our messaging platform, and the carriers that deliver the message. They may use your number only to carry out that work for us, and may not use it for anything of their own. LeadConnector's own privacy policy covers its relationship with us as its customer; these terms and our Privacy Policy are what govern the number you gave us.

The Privacy Policy at https://blueobsidian.io/legal/privacy describes everything else we hold, how long we keep it, and how to ask for a copy or to have it deleted. It carries this same no-sharing promise in its section 6a. Ask and we delete it - unsubscribing keeps only the record that you unsubscribed, which is what stops us contacting you again by mistake.

7. Carriers and handsets

The program is supported on the major US mobile carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Messages may not arrive if your handset is off, out of range, out of storage, or if your carrier or plan blocks them.

8. Contact

Questions about these messages, a request to stop them, or a request to see what we hold: hello@blueobsidian.io. We answer messaging and privacy requests within thirty days and usually within one working day.

If we change what these messages contain or how often they arrive, we update this page and change the date at the top of it. A change that widens the program beyond what section 2 describes needs fresh consent from you rather than a new version of this page.
