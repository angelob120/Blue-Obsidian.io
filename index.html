Questions

The ones owners ask first.

So what does it actually cost me?

Nothing. There's no subscription and we take no cut of your sales. A small service fee is added to the customer's order at checkout, shown to them before they pay, and that's what funds the platform. You still cover your own card fees, same as always.

Won't a fee scare customers off?

It's sized to disappear: about $1-2 on a normal order, and it only scales up on unusually large ones. Fees on a phone checkout are familiar now, and it's shown as its own line rather than buried in the prices.

I already add my own surcharge. Does this stack?

It can, and we'd rather talk about it upfront than surprise you. Our fee stays small and proportional. If you're already running a card surcharge, we'll walk through how the order reads before you launch.

Do I need a new POS or tablet?

No POS, and no printer unless you want one. Orders arrive as an email, so the laptop or phone you already own is a complete setup you can finish on your own tonight. What we recommend on top is a tablet on a stand by the pass showing nothing but orders - use a spare tablet if you have one, or the pair we recommend is about $75 from Amazon, bought by you at Amazon's price. We do not resell them and make nothing on them. Nothing to install either way, and a free Android app for the tablet is coming.

How do customers find my ordering page?

You paste your link into your Google Business Profile, your Instagram bio, and a QR code on the counter. When someone taps “Order online” on your Google listing, your page opens - your logo, your menu, your prices, with nobody in between.

Can I leave and take my customer list?

Yes. Export it any time, including on your way out. Owning that list is the entire point - holding it hostage would defeat it.
