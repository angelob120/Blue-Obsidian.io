Straight answers

What you are probably wondering.

It is free? What is the catch?

There is a fee. It is just not on your side. A small service fee of about $1 to $2 is added to your customer's order at checkout, and they see it on their receipt before they pay. That is what pays for the whole thing. You pay no monthly bill, and we take none of your sales.

Will the fee cost me orders?

It is smaller than what a delivery app already costs them. Restaurants raise their prices on those apps to cover the 15 to 30 percent cut, so your customer pays more there and never gets told why. A dollar or two, shown on the receipt, is cheaper and honest about it.

I already use DoorDash and Uber Eats.

Keep them. They are good at finding you new people. This is for the customers who already know you and would have called or walked in, and who cost you a third of the order to serve on an app. Most restaurants use both, and move their regulars over slowly.

Do I need a new POS, tablet or printer?

No. Orders show up on the phone, tablet or laptop you already have, in the same browser you read this on, and that alone is the whole thing working, free. What we recommend on top is a tablet on a stand by the pass showing nothing but orders - a spare tablet you already own works, or the pair we recommend is about $75 from Amazon, bought by you at Amazon's price. A kitchen printer is optional on top and you buy that one too. A free Android app for the tablet is coming.

I am not good with computers, and I have no time.

Adding your menu is typing in food and prices. Start with your five best sellers and add the rest whenever. If you would rather not do it at all, send us your menu and we will build the page for you.

What if I want to leave?

You download your customer list and go. No contract, no notice, and no phone call to cancel. The whole point is that the list is yours, so keeping it from you would make no sense.
